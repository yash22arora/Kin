## Description
Everyone you love is a light.

Kin turns your relationships into a private night sky. Every person you
care about becomes a star. Log a small moment together — a call, a coffee,
a laugh — and their star brightens. Quiet weeks let it soften, the way
distance really feels. It never goes dark, because distance isn't failure.
It's just distance.

Friends who share moments become constellations. People you see twice a
year keep their glow for months — you choose each person's orbit, so Kin
never guilts you about someone you're fine with. There are no streaks, no
scores, no red badges. Just light, where the light has been.

FIVE SECONDS A DAY
Tap a star, write a line (or don't), done. A shooting star carries your
moment home. That's the whole ritual.

THE WIDGET IS THE POINT
Your sky lives on your home screen, glowing at a glance — or pin one
person's star and keep them close. The sky deepens with the real time of
day, and if you're lucky, you'll catch a meteor.

YOURS ALONE
No accounts. No feeds. No ads. Your sky never leaves your device — every
name, note, and photo stays with you. Face ID lock if you want it. Export
everything, anytime.

REMEMBER, GENTLY
Stars for people you've lost can be marked Remembered — they hold steady
forever, and never dim.

ALSO
• Tell Siri: "Log a moment with Mom in Kin"
• A daily stargazing reminder, only if you ask — "The sky is out."
• Drag stars to arrange your own sky
• Full VoiceOver support; every animation respects Reduce Motion

THE DEAL, PLAINLY
Kin is free for 7 days, fully. Then one price, once, forever — or keep a
free sky of three stars. No subscription. No account. Stars beyond three
rest; they're never deleted, and they return the moment you unlock.

Every day counts. So does everyone.
